# CLB Reusable Commander Draft Cube Guide

## Purpose and rules boundary

This guide uses five *Commander Legends: Battle for Baldur's Gate* (CLB) Prerelease Kits to build a reusable Commander Draft cube for 3, 4, or 5 players. The 15 UrzaGatherer booster CSV exports are the authoritative inventory for the 300 cube cards; the separate promo export is not part of that count.

The guide distinguishes **official Commander Limited rules** from **house procedures for this reusable cube**. Session Pool selection, Resting Pool rotation, category balancing, reconstructed packs, and event sideboards are house procedures. They are not official Wizards collation or tournament rules.

## Official Commander Limited rules

For a CLB Commander Draft:

- Each player starts with three boosters and drafts two cards per pick.
- Drafted duplicates are allowed.
- Build a Commander deck of at least 60 cards, including the commander and any Background used. Add basic lands to the deck; they do not go on top of the minimum.
- For multiplayer Commander, each player starts at 40 life.
- Every card in the deck must fit the commander's color identity.

Wizards' CLB Prerelease guidance says to add the year-stamped promo to the player's draft pool before drafting. It also recommends gameplay pods as close to four players as possible. Those instructions describe the official event; the home-cube promo assignment and seating options below are house choices.

## The 300-card cube

The inventory is expanded from the CSV `Deck count` values, not from the number of CSV rows. Each of the 15 booster exports totals 20 cards, for 300 cards overall. The five cards in `CLB-Promos.csv` are maintained separately.

The pool is not evenly divided by color. For smaller events, use proportional selection and preserve access to all five colors, fixing, Backgrounds, and commanders rather than forcing identical color counts into every reconstructed pack.

## House procedure: reusable-cube terms

**Master Pool:** all 300 cards from the 15 booster exports. Promos are separate.

**Session Pool:** the cards selected from the Master Pool for one event.

**Resting Pool:** Master Pool cards not used in that event. They remain part of the cube.

**Reconstructed booster:** a house-assembled 20-card pack from the Session Pool. It is not a factory-collated Wizards booster.

**Event card pool:** cards a player drafted plus their assigned promo. Under this house procedure, drafted cards not in the current deck remain available between games.

## House procedure: select a Session Pool

| Players | Packs | Session Pool | Resting Pool |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 9 | 180 | 120 |
| 4 | 12 | 240 | 60 |
| 5 | 15 | 300 | 0 |

Each player receives three packs. Sort the Master Pool into functional groups before selecting cards:

1. Backgrounds
2. Commander-capable cards other than Backgrounds
3. Other rares and mythics
4. Other uncommons
5. White, blue, black, red, and green commons
6. Common utility cards, including colorless cards, artifacts, lands, Gates, and fixing

For 3 players, aim for roughly 60% of the pool; for 4 players, roughly 80%; for 5 players, use all cards. The exact category targets in the workbook are starting points, not a substitute for checking the final count. Adjust the selections to exactly 180 or 240 cards.

**Pack-level guarantees for this house format:**

- Include at least one Background in every pack.
- Include at least one commander-capable non-Background card in every pack.
- Keep all five colors represented in the Session Pool.
- Keep utility and fixing in reasonable proportion to the full pool.
- For 3- and 4-player events, vary which cards are selected.

The unused cards form the Resting Pool. Before the next event, return them to their categories, shuffle each category, and make a fresh selection. This rotation is a house procedure intended to avoid a permanent “A team” and “B team.”

## House procedure: reconstruct the packs

Create 9, 12, or 15 empty pack positions for 3, 4, or 5 players. Use this sequence to balance the selected cards; it does not attempt to reproduce Wizards' print-run collation:

1. Shuffle the selected Backgrounds and place one in each pack.
2. Shuffle the selected commander-capable cards and place one in each pack.
3. Distribute bonus commanders and other rares/mythics as evenly as the pool allows.
4. Shuffle each color's commons separately and snake-deal them across packs: deal pack 1 through pack N, then pack N back through pack 1, reversing direction on each pass.
5. Snake-deal utility and fixing, then ordinary uncommons.
6. Deal remaining eligible Session Pool cards until each pack has exactly 20 cards.
7. Shuffle the completed packs and give each player three.

## Draft and deck construction

### Official draft structure

Give each player three boosters. Players choose two cards at a time from the pack they open, then pass the remaining cards. Reverse the passing direction for the next booster. Duplicates are permitted in Commander Draft.

### House promo assignment

For this reusable event, randomly assign one of the five separately tracked promos to each player before drafting. A 3- or 4-player event will leave some promos unused; return all promos to the separate promo pool during cleanup. The official CLB Prerelease instruction to add a player's year-stamped promo before drafting is described above; random assignment from this shared collection is the cube's house method.

### Official deck construction and gameplay

Build a deck of **at least 60 cards**, including the commander and any Background used, with basic lands included in that total. The 60-card figure is a minimum, not a required exact size. Deck cards must meet the chosen commander's color-identity requirements. Start multiplayer Commander games at 40 life.

The official CLB Prerelease recommendation is to seat gameplay pods as close to four players as possible. For a home event, a three-player free-for-all or a five-player game are reasonable house options when attendance makes a four-player pod impractical.

## House procedure: sideboards and resetting the cube

Between games, players may rebuild using only their drafted event card pool and assigned promo. The rebuilt deck must remain a legal Commander Draft deck of at least 60 cards. No card may be added from another player's pool or the Resting Pool; basic lands may be adjusted freely. This event-sideboard procedure is a house rule, not an official sideboard rule.

After the final game:

1. Collect cube cards from decks and event card pools.
2. Collect all five promos separately.
3. Remove borrowed basic lands.
4. Verify 300 Master Pool cards and 5 separate promos.
5. Return the cube cards to their sorting groups for the next Session Pool.

## Quick setup checklist

**Before the draft (house cube setup):**

- [ ] Choose 3, 4, or 5 players.
- [ ] Select exactly 180, 240, or 300 Session Pool cards.
- [ ] Set aside the Resting Pool.
- [ ] Build 9, 12, or 15 packs of exactly 20 cards each.
- [ ] Check every pack for a Background and a commander-capable card.
- [ ] Shuffle the completed packs and prepare promos separately.

**At the draft and games:**

- [ ] Assign promos using the house procedure.
- [ ] Give each player three packs and draft two cards per pick.
- [ ] Build a Commander deck of at least 60 cards, lands included.
- [ ] Start multiplayer Commander games at 40 life.
- [ ] Keep unused drafted cards with the player's event pool between games (house procedure).

**At cleanup:**

- [ ] Return cube cards and all promos to their separate pools.
- [ ] Remove basic lands.
- [ ] Verify the totals: 300 cube cards and 5 promos.
